About Me

Hi, I'm Rahel.

My path to becoming a doula began with my own birth story. When I welcomed my first child, I had the profound gift of being supported by a doula — and it changed everything. In what could have been an overwhelming experience, I felt calm, informed, and deeply cared for.

I knew then that I wanted to give other families that same feeling — that reassuring sense that you are not alone. Based in Toronto, I offer non-judgmental, inclusive, and evidence-based care.

How I can support you

Prenatal Support

Postpartum Care
