Hello & Welcome

Hi, I'm Rahel.

My path to becoming a doula began with my own birth story. When I welcomed my first child, I had the profound gift of being supported by a doula — and it changed everything. In what could have been an overwhelming experience, I felt calm, informed, and deeply cared for.

I knew then that I wanted to give other families that same feeling — that reassuring sense that you are not alone, that someone is truly in your corner. My goal is to ensure you feel heard, informed, and deeply supported at every step of your journey.

Your Journey, Grounded in Serenity

Finding the right support allows you to navigate life’s most profound transitions with confidence. A doula doesn’t replace your team; they provide the dedicated presence that allows you to feel safe, heard, and empowered.

My approach centers inclusive, evidence-based care tailored to your unique family.

How I can support you

Prenatal Support

Preparing you for the profound changes ahead through information, birth planning, and regular check-ins.

  • Prenatal visits (in-person or virtual)
  • Pregnancy nutrition & wellness
  • Birth preference mapping

Postpartum Care

Continuous support to help you rest, heal, and find your own rhythm as a new family.

  • Newborn care education
  • Breast/chest-feeding support
  • Home & recovery support
